Title: A 25-Foot Wave of Molasses Hit Boston 🍯🌊 (True Story)

Boston, January 1919. A tank of over 2 million gallons of molasses burst, and a sticky wave chased people down the street. 😱

🌊 25 feet high, moving at about 35 mph
🔩 The tank leaked from day one… so they painted it brown
🌡️ Warm molasses + freezing air = a deadly trap
⚖️ It changed the law: big tanks now need an engineer's signature

💬 Did you know this really happened?

Sources: Wikipedia "Great Molasses Flood", Live Science (2016), New England Historical Society, Harvard fluid dynamics study (2016)
